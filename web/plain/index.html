The short version長話短說

On Friday 28 August 2026, Hims & Hers stock closed at 28.84 US dollars in New York. Hims & Hers is a US health company. Two days later, on Sunday night, a token for that same stock traded at about 71 on a blockchain. That is about 2.5 times the real price.

Nothing had happened to the company. Over the weekend, no new tokens could be made. Then buyers of a memecoin called BONER took almost all the HIMS out of the main trading pool. With the shelf empty, small purchases pushed the price way up. On Monday new tokens arrived, and about 75 minutes later the price was back.

Why does it matter? An app that lends or pays out on this price would have treated each token as worth about 71. Worse, the price had become almost free to push around on purpose.

HAKARI is a safety check for moments like this. It spots when a price is cheap to fake compared with the money riding on it. Then it refuses to pay out. This pool never had HAKARI. In a replay of the weekend, it would have refused.

2026 年 8 月最後一個週末，美股 HIMS（Hims & Hers）週五收在 28.84 美元。可是在 Robinhood 的區塊鏈上，代表這檔股票的代幣，週日深夜一度被報到約 71。這差不多是收盤價的 2.5 倍。

這跟公司本身無關。週末做不出新代幣，偏偏又有一個叫 BONER 的迷因幣，把主要交易池裡的 HIMS 幾乎買光。貨架一空，一點小買單就能把價格推得很高。週一新代幣一到，大約 75 分鐘，價格就回到原位。

為什麼重要？如果有借貸或結算的應用照這個價格算錢，每顆 HIMS 就會被當成約 71。更糟的是，當時要故意把價格推高，幾乎不用花錢。

HAKARI 就是為這種時刻設計的安全檢查：價格很便宜就能造假、押在上面的錢卻很多時，它就拒絕付錢。那個池子當時沒有裝 HAKARI；我們拿真實交易重播一遍，它會拒絕。

Prices on the blockchain are in USDG, a dollar stablecoin: a token built to track the US dollar. The Friday close of 28.84 is in real US dollars. All times are UTC (Taipei is 8 hours ahead, Tokyo 9).鏈上價格都以 USDG 計；USDG 是一種美元穩定幣，也就是設計來跟著美元走的代幣。週五收盤價 28.84 是真正的美元。本頁時間都是 UTC（世界標準時間），台灣時間請加 8 小時。

A token that stands for a real share一顆代幣，對應一股真股票

Robinhood, the US trading app, runs its own blockchain called Robinhood Chain. On it, people can hold tokens that stand for shares of real companies. HIMS is the token for one share of Hims & Hers.

Robinhood 是美國的券商 App，自己經營一條區塊鏈，叫 Robinhood Chain。這裡的 HIMS 是「代幣化股票」：Robinhood 在這條鏈上發行的代幣，每一顆對應一股真正的 Hims & Hers 股票。

Robinhood makes new tokens backed by real shares it holds. It removes tokens when people cash them in. But it can only do this while its stock trading is open. So from Friday night until the first new tokens on Monday, the number of HIMS tokens was stuck at about 15,227.

Think of a shop that cannot restock on weekends. If a crowd shows up on Sunday, the shelf can empty. No truck is coming until Monday.

Robinhood 手上有真股票，才會做出對應的新代幣（正式說法：鑄造）。有人把代幣交回去，代幣就收掉（正式說法：贖回）。但這兩件事，只能在 Robinhood 的股票交易時段內做。

所以週末就像倉庫關門的店：架上有多少就是多少，不能補貨，也不能退貨。從週五晚上到週一第一批新代幣出現，鏈上的 HIMS 一直固定在約 15,227 顆。

Over the weekend, no new HIMS tokens could be made. The supply was frozen.平日缺貨可以補；週末數量是死的，只有約 15,227 顆。

For the curious想多知道一點

Making and cashing in tokens (minting and redeeming) follows Robinhood's round-the-clock weekday hours, called 24/5. This weekend they were shut from Sat 00:00 to Mon 00:00 UTC. On Friday evening, 900 HIMS were cashed in. That took the count from about 16,127 to about 15,227. It stayed there until Monday's first new tokens.

做新代幣和收回代幣，跟著 Robinhood 的「24/5」平日全天交易時段走：紐約時間週日晚上 8 點，到週五晚上 8 點。這個週末關閉的時間，是 UTC 週六 00:00 到週一 00:00。週五晚上有 900 顆被換回，總數從約 16,127 降到約 15,227，一直停到週一第一批新代幣。

A memecoin that had to buy HIMS first一個得先買 HIMS 的迷因幣

A memecoin is a joke token that people trade for fun and fast gains. One, called BONER, was priced in HIMS instead of dollars.

BONER 是一個迷因幣，也就是靠話題和社群炒起來的代幣。它有個特別的地方：不是用美元標價，而是用 HIMS 標價。

Most people buy HIMS from one main dollar pool: a shared pot of HIMS and USDG that anyone can trade against. The more HIMS people take out, the higher the price of the next one. Think of this pool as the shelf in our shop.

To buy BONER with dollars, you first bought HIMS off that shelf. Then you swapped the HIMS for BONER. That HIMS then sat in BONER's own pool until someone sold BONER. By Monday 00:29 UTC, BONER's pool held 12,877 HIMS, about 85% of all HIMS tokens.

大家用美元買賣 HIMS 的地方，是一個「大家交易用的共同資金池」（流動性池），裡面放著 HIMS 和 USDG，誰都能跟它交易。池子裡的 HIMS 被拿走越多，下一顆就越貴。可以把它想成店裡的貨架。

所以用美元買 BONER，主要路線是先用美元買進 HIMS，再拿 HIMS 去換 BONER。每一筆這樣的買單，都會順手帶走一些 HIMS。這些 HIMS 進了 BONER 的池子，要等有人賣 BONER 才會出來。到週一 00:29（UTC），BONER 的池子握有 12,877 顆 HIMS，約是全部 HIMS 的 85%。

People were buying a memecoin, but the stock token is what got carried off.大家買的是迷因幣，被搬走的卻是股票代幣。

For the curious想多知道一點

BONER also had a smaller pool priced directly in USDG. But its main market was the one priced in HIMS, so most dollar buying went through HIMS first.

BONER 另有一個直接用美元交易的池子，但比較小。它的主要市場是用 HIMS 標價的那個，所以大部分用美元的買單，都得先經過 HIMS。

An empty shelf makes a wild price貨架一空，價格就亂跳

A pool's price depends on what is left in it. With almost no HIMS left, each small purchase moved the price a lot. At 23:31 UTC on Sunday, the price closed that minute at 71.23. The real stock had closed at 28.84.

這種池子的定價規則很單純：架上剩越少，價格就喊越高。HIMS 只剩幾顆時，每一筆小買單都能把價格推動很多。23:31（UTC）這一分鐘，池子收在 71.23，是週五收盤價 28.84 的 2.5 倍左右。

On a normal weekday, new tokens would flow in and pull a wild price back down. This weekend, nobody could make new tokens. So the wild price just stayed.

平常的日子，新代幣會流進來，把離譜的價格拉回去。這個週末沒有人能做出新代幣，所以離譜的價格就一直掛在那裡。

You may have seen “$132” on X你可能在 X 上看過「132 美元」

That number spread from a news story the next day. In the main pool, nobody bought anywhere near that price. After one small purchase, the pool's price read 124.70 for an instant. 這個數字，是隔天一篇新聞報導傳開的。主要美元池裡，沒有人用接近這個價格買到。有一筆小買單成交後，池子的報價一瞬間跳到 124.70；但那筆買單平均每顆只付了約 71。確實有一筆約半顆的小交易，以約 133 成交，但那是在一個收 50% 手續費的小池子裡。本頁用每一分鐘結束時的價格，最高是 71.23。

The blockchain price hit about two and a half times the real one, and nobody could fix it until Monday.鏈上價格衝到真實股價的約 2.5 倍，而週一之前沒有人能把它拉回來。

更麻煩的是，價格變得很好推。我們的測法是：買一筆把價格推高 10%，再馬上賣回去。大部分的錢會拿回來，虧掉的是手續費；這筆虧損就是成本。

If an app read this price · Sun 23:31 UTC如果有應用讀了這個價格 · 週日 23:31（UTC）

Apps could have paid out on that price應用程式可能照這個價格付錢

Some blockchain apps read prices straight from pools. Lending apps use the price to value what borrowers put up. Options and perpetual futures, which are bets on where a price goes, use it to decide who gets paid. We are not saying any app read this pool. The cards show what one would have done if it had.

有些區塊鏈應用會直接讀池子的價格來算錢，例如借貸（拿代幣抵押借錢）、選擇權（押漲跌），還有永續合約（不會到期的期貨）。我們不是說真的有應用讀了這個池子；下面三張卡，是假如有的話，會發生什麼事。

On Sunday night, any app reading this pool would have seen HIMS at up to about 71. That was real trading, not a glitch. But it was far from the stock's value. Nobody could correct it until Monday.

Nobody had to cheat for this to happen. One post online called it a short squeeze. That is when traders betting on a fall are forced to buy back. But nobody needed to be betting against HIMS here. As @0xSammy put it on X, “It’s a float squeeze not a short squeeze.” The float is the number of tokens free to trade, and it was stuck.

Who lost? According to DeFiPrime, mostly late buyers. People who bought HIMS at 40 to 60 USDG on Sunday night held a token worth about 29 a few hours later.

Worse, by late Sunday a 10% push cost only about 12 USDG. So someone could push the price up on purpose, then borrow or collect far more than the push cost. That is what faking a price means. It is what HAKARI checks for.

如果有應用讀的是這個池子，那個週日深夜它會讀到：一檔收盤 28.84 的股票，值 71。這是真的成交，不是系統出錯；但它離股票的真實價值很遠。週一之前，沒有人能把它拉回來。

這不需要任何人作弊。網路上有貼文把它說成「軋空」：押股價下跌的人被迫買回。但這裡根本不需要有人押 HIMS 下跌。就像 @0xSammy 在 X 上說的：「這是流通量擠壓，不是軋空。」（原文：“It’s a float squeeze not a short squeeze”）流通量，就是能自由交易的代幣數量；它被卡住了。

誰吃虧了？照 DeFiPrime 的分析，多半是高點追買的人：週日深夜用 40 到 60 USDG 買進，幾小時後只剩約 29。

更危險的是，週日深夜把價格推高 10%，只要約 12 USDG。有心人可以花小錢故意把價格推高，讓借貸應用多借給他、讓選擇權多賠給他。這就叫價格「造假」，也正是 HAKARI 要防的事。

A price can come from honest trading and still be unsafe to pay out on.價格是真的成交出來的，不代表它能拿來付錢。

For the curious想多知道一點

HAKARI calls a pool like this “fenced”. The trading that usually pulls the price back cannot reach it. So a pushed price stays put. Apps often smooth prices with an average over time (a TWAP). That only slows things down. In our replay, the 30-minute average at 23:31 was still 45.34, more than half above 28.84. This pool never kept an average like that, so the figure is worked out after the fact.

HAKARI 把這種池子叫做「被圍起來的」（fenced）：平常把價格拉回股價的交易進不來，價格被推上去就停在那裡。應用讀價格的管道叫「預言機」（oracle），常用一段時間的平均價（TWAP）來防亂跳，但平均價只是慢一點跟上。我們事後重播推算：23:31 的 30 分鐘平均價仍有 45.34，比收盤價 28.84 高五成多。這是推算，池子當時其實沒裝這種紀錄器。

Check the cost of faking before paying out付錢之前，先秤一秤

HAKARI is Japanese for a weighing scale. It is a safety check that apps can run before they pay out on a price. It asks: how much would it cost to fake this price right now? From that, it works out a safe amount: the most money that can safely depend on the price. A smaller payout goes ahead. A bigger one, HAKARI refuses.

HAKARI 是日文的「秤」。應用要照某個價格付錢之前，可以先問它：現在要把這個價格做假，得花多少錢？它從這個答案算出一個「安全金額」，也就是最多能有多少錢押在這個價格上。要付的錢比它少，就照付；比它多，HAKARI 就拒絕。

HAKARI tries pushes of several sizes and uses the one that is the best deal for a cheater. At 23:25 on Sunday, that was a push of about 20%, costing about 4.08 USDG. A smaller push costs less, but it also wins less. A price faked 20% too high makes every 1 USDG that depends on it pay out 0.20 USDG too much. So the safe amount was 4.08 ÷ 0.20, about 20.38 USDG.

HAKARI 會試好幾種推高的幅度，挑對作弊者最划算的那一種。週日 23:25，最划算的是把價格推高約 20%，只要約 4.08 USDG。推得少一點更便宜，但能多拿的錢也更少。價格被假造高了 20%，押在這個價格上的每 1 USDG，就會多付 0.20 USDG。所以安全金額是 4.08 ÷ 0.20，約 20.38 USDG。

This pool never had HAKARI. We replayed that weekend's real trades through HAKARI's rules, minute by minute. So this is a what-if replay, not a record. In the replay, the safe amount was about 7,259 USDG at 19:40 on Sunday. By 23:25, it had fallen to about 20.

So, in the replay, a payout worth 1,000 USDG would have been refused for 183 minutes, about three hours in all. The longest stretch ran two hours, from 22:51 to 00:50. Our first version of the rule would have paid out in those minutes, at prices up to 51.70. That is nearly 80% above Friday's close. An internal review caught this, and we replaced that version.

The new rule has a limit too. HAKARI checks how cheap a price is to fake. It does not know what the stock is worth. In the replay, new tokens had made the pool deeper again by 00:51 on Monday. So HAKARI would have let a 1,000 USDG payout through, at its 30-minute average price of 50.31. The pool itself was already down to 32.71.

Even on a calm evening, this small pool could safely carry only about 7,259 USDG in the replay. So a 10,000 USDG payout would have been refused anyway. That is the pool's normal size, not the squeeze.

先講清楚：那個池子當時沒有裝 HAKARI。我們是拿它那晚真實的每一筆交易，套上 HAKARI 的規則逐分鐘重跑一遍，所以這是重播推演，不是紀錄。重播中，安全金額從 19:40 的約 7,259 USDG，掉到 23:25 只剩約 20 USDG。

換成一筆 1,000 USDG 的結算：在重播中，HAKARI 會拒絕 183 分鐘，加起來大約三小時。最長一次從 22:51 到 00:50，連續兩小時。我們第一版的規則，在這些分鐘裡會照付，價格最高算到 51.70，比週五收盤價高了將近八成。內部審查抓到這個問題，那一版已經換掉。

新規則也有它的極限。HAKARI 算的是造假有多便宜，不是股票值多少。在重播中，到了週一 00:51，新代幣已經讓池子變深，HAKARI 就會放行一筆 1,000 USDG 的結算。它用的是 30 分鐘平均價 50.31，但那時池子本身的價格已經掉到 32.71。

在重播中，即使是平靜的傍晚，這個小池子也只能安全承載約 7,259 USDG。所以 10,000 USDG 的結算，本來就會被拒絕。那是它平常的深度，不是擠壓造成的。

HAKARI says no when faking the price would cost less than a cheater could win from it.HAKARI 不猜價格對不對，只算造假划不划算：造假的成本比能多拿的錢還少，它就拒絕。
