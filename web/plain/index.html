The short version長話短說

On Friday 28 August 2026, Hims & Hers stock closed at 28.84 US dollars in New York. Hims & Hers is a US health company. Two days later, on Sunday night, a token for that same stock traded at about 71 on a blockchain.

Nothing had happened to the company. The token had simply run short over a weekend, when no new tokens could be made. On Monday new tokens arrived, and about 75 minutes later the price was back.

HAKARI is a safety check for moments like this. It spots when a price is cheap to fake compared with the money riding on it. Then it refuses to pay out.

2026 年 8 月最後一個週末，美股 HIMS（Hims & Hers）週五收在 28.84 美元。可是在 Robinhood 的區塊鏈上，代表這檔股票的代幣，週日深夜一度被報到約 71。這差不多是收盤價的 2.5 倍。

這跟公司本身無關。原因是週末補不了貨，偏偏又有一個迷因幣把貨架搬空。週一一補貨，大約 75 分鐘，價格就回到原位。

HAKARI 就是為這種時刻設計的安全檢查。它會看出：這個價格要造假很便宜，押在上面的錢卻很多。這時它就拒絕付錢。

Prices on the blockchain are in USDG, a dollar stablecoin: a token built to track the US dollar. The Friday close of 28.84 is in real US dollars. All times are UTC (Taipei is 8 hours ahead, Tokyo 9).鏈上價格都以 USDG 計；USDG 是一種美元穩定幣，也就是設計來跟著美元走的代幣。週五收盤價 28.84 是真正的美元。本頁時間都是 UTC（世界標準時間），台灣時間請加 8 小時。

A token that stands for a real share一顆代幣，對應一股真股票

Robinhood, the US trading app, runs its own blockchain called Robinhood Chain. On it, people can hold tokens that stand for shares of real companies. HIMS is the token for one share of Hims & Hers.

Robinhood 是美國的券商 App，自己經營一條區塊鏈，叫 Robinhood Chain。這裡的 HIMS 是「代幣化股票」：Robinhood 在這條鏈上發行的代幣，每一顆對應一股真正的 Hims & Hers 股票。

Robinhood makes new tokens backed by real shares it holds. It removes tokens when people cash them in. But it can only do this while its stock trading is open. So from Friday night until the first new tokens on Monday, the number of HIMS tokens was stuck at about 15,227.

Think of a shop that cannot restock on weekends. If a crowd shows up on Sunday, the shelf can empty. No truck is coming until Monday.

要多一顆代幣，發行方得先在券商那邊買進股票，才能新增代幣（正式說法：鑄造）。代幣也能交回去換掉（正式說法：贖回）。但這兩件事，只有券商那一端在交易時才做得到。

所以週末就像倉庫關門的店：架上有多少就是多少，不能補貨，也不能退貨。從週五晚上到週一第一批新代幣出現，鏈上的 HIMS 一直固定在約 15,227 顆。

Over the weekend, no new HIMS tokens could be made. The supply was frozen.平日缺貨可以補；週末數量是死的，只有約 15,227 顆。

Making and cashing in tokens (minting and redeeming) follows Robinhood's 24/5 session. This weekend that window was shut from Sat 00:00 to Mon 00:00 UTC. On Friday evening, 900 HIMS were cashed in. That took the count from about 16,127 to about 15,227, where it stayed until Monday's first new tokens.

新增／換回跟著 Robinhood 的「24/5」交易時段走：紐約時間週日晚上 8 點，到週五晚上 8 點。這個週末關閉的時間，是 UTC 週六 00:00 到週一 00:00。週五晚上有 900 顆被換回，總數從約 16,127 降到約 15,227，一直停到週一第一批新代幣。

A memecoin that had to buy HIMS first一個得先買 HIMS 的迷因幣

A memecoin is a joke token that people trade for fun and fast gains. One, called BONER, was priced in HIMS instead of dollars.

BONER 是一個迷因幣，也就是靠話題和社群炒起來的代幣。它有個特別的地方：不是用美元標價，而是用 HIMS 標價。

Most people buy HIMS from one main dollar pool: a shared pot of HIMS and USDG that anyone can trade against. The more HIMS people take out, the higher the price of the next one. Think of this pool as the shelf in our shop.

To buy BONER with dollars, you first bought HIMS off that shelf. Then you swapped the HIMS for BONER. That HIMS then sat in BONER's own pool until someone sold BONER. By Monday 00:29 UTC, BONER's pool held 12,877 HIMS, about 85% of all HIMS tokens.

大家用美元買賣 HIMS 的地方，是一個「大家交易用的共同資金池」（流動性池），裡面放著 HIMS 和 USDG，誰都能跟它交易。池子裡的 HIMS 被拿走越多，下一顆就越貴。可以把它想成店裡的貨架。

所以用美元買 BONER，主要路線是先用美元買進 HIMS，再拿 HIMS 去換 BONER。每一筆這樣的買單，都會順手帶走一些 HIMS。這些 HIMS 進了 BONER 的池子，要等有人賣 BONER 才會出來。到週一 00:29（UTC），BONER 的池子握有 12,877 顆 HIMS，約是全部 HIMS 的 85%。

People were buying a memecoin, but the stock token is what got carried off.大家買的是迷因幣，被搬走的卻是股票代幣。

An empty shelf makes a wild price貨架一空，價格就亂跳

A pool's price depends on what is left in it. With almost no HIMS left, each small purchase moved the price a lot. At 23:31 UTC on Sunday, the price closed that minute at 71.23. The real stock had closed at 28.84.

這種池子的定價規則很單純：架上剩越少，價格就喊越高。HIMS 只剩幾顆時，每一筆小買單都能把價格推動很多。23:31（UTC）這一分鐘，池子收在 71.23，是週五收盤價 28.84 的 2.5 倍左右。

On a normal weekday, new tokens would flow in and pull a wild price back down. This weekend, nobody could make new tokens. So the wild price just stayed.

平常的日子，新代幣會流進來，把離譜的價格拉回去。這個週末沒有人能做出新代幣，所以離譜的價格就一直掛在那裡。

No trade in the two main dollar pools came near that. For an instant the pool's price read 124.70. A buyer had paid 71.25 per token on average, with almost nothing left to buy. This page uses the price at the end of each minute; its highest was 71.23.

兩個主要美元池裡，沒有任何成交接近這個數字。池子的報價曾有一瞬間停在 124.70：那筆買單平均每顆只付 71.25，而且當時幾乎已經沒貨可買。本頁用每一分鐘結束時的價格，最高是 71.23。

The blockchain price hit about two and a half times the real one, and nobody could fix it until Monday.鏈上價格衝到真實股價的約 2.5 倍，而週一之前沒有人能把它拉回來。

Monday: the shelf is restocked週一補貨，價格回原位

Robinhood's trading session reopened at 00:00 UTC on Monday. 43 minutes later, at 00:43:30, the first new HIMS tokens since Friday arrived: 1,000 of them. A trading wallet then sold the new tokens into the pools as they came in.

週一 00:00（UTC，紐約還是週日晚上 8 點），券商交易時段重新開張。43 分鐘後，第一批新的 HIMS 在 00:43:30 上鏈，一次 1,000 顆。新貨一到，發行方那邊就把它賣進池子。

If an app read this price · Sun 23:31 UTC如果有應用讀了這個價格 · 週日 23:31（UTC）

Apps could have paid out on that price應用程式可能照這個價格付錢

Many blockchain apps read prices straight from pools like this one. Lending apps use the price to value what borrowers put up. Options and perpetual futures, which are bets on where a price goes, use it to decide who gets paid.

很多區塊鏈應用會直接讀池子的價格來算錢。例如借貸（拿代幣抵押借錢）、選擇權（押漲跌），還有永續合約（不會到期的期貨）。

On Sunday night, any app reading this pool would have seen HIMS at up to about 71. That was real trading, not a glitch. But it was far from the stock's value, and it was cheap to push further. Nobody could correct it until Monday.

Nobody had to cheat for this to happen. One post online pitched it as a short squeeze, where traders betting on a fall are forced to buy back. But nobody needed to be betting against HIMS here. As @0xSammy put it on X, “It’s a float squeeze not a short squeeze.” The float is the number of tokens free to trade, and it was stuck.

Who lost? According to DeFiPrime, mostly late buyers. People who bought HIMS at 40 to 60 USDG on Sunday night held a token worth about 29 a few hours later.

如果有應用讀的是這個池子，那個週日深夜它會讀到：一檔收盤 28.84 的股票，值 71。這是真的成交，不是系統出錯；但它離股票的真實價值很遠，而且很便宜就能再推高。週一之前，沒有人能把它拉回來。

這不需要任何人作弊。網路上有貼文把它說成「軋空」：押股價下跌的人被迫買回。但這裡根本不需要有人押 HIMS 下跌。就像 @0xSammy 在 X 上說的：「這是流通量擠壓，不是軋空。」（原文：“It’s a float squeeze not a short squeeze”）流通量，就是能自由交易的代幣數量；它被卡住了。

誰吃虧了？照 DeFiPrime 的分析，多半是高點追買的人：週日深夜用 40 到 60 USDG 買進，幾小時後只剩約 29。

A price can come from honest trading and still be unsafe to pay out on.價格是真的成交出來的，不代表它能拿來付錢。

For the curious想多知道一點

HAKARI calls a pool like this “fenced”: the trading that normally pulls its price back to the stock's cannot reach it, so a push stays put. Apps often smooth prices with an average over time (a TWAP). That only slows things down. In our replay, the 30-minute average at 23:31 was still 45.34, more than half above 28.84. This pool never had a price recorder like that, so the figure is worked out after the fact.

HAKARI 把這種池子叫做「被圍起來的」（fenced）：平常把價格拉回股價的交易進不來，價格被推上去就停在那裡。應用讀價格的管道叫「預言機」（oracle），常用一段時間的平均價（TWAP）來防亂跳，但平均價只是慢一點跟上。我們事後重播推算：23:31 的 30 分鐘平均價仍有 45.34，比收盤價 28.84 高五成多。這是推算，池子當時其實沒裝這種紀錄器。

What-if replay · Sun 30 Aug 18:00 → Mon 31 Aug 10:00 UTC重播推演 · 8/30（日）18:00 → 8/31（一）10:00（UTC）

Check the cost of faking before paying out付錢之前，先秤一秤

HAKARI is Japanese for a weighing scale. It is a safety check that apps can run before they pay out on a price. It asks two questions. How much would it cost to fake this price right now? And how much money is riding on it? It turns the answers into one number, the safe amount. Below it, the payout goes ahead. Above it, HAKARI says no.

HAKARI 是日文的「秤」。一筆錢要照某個價格結算前，它先問兩件事：現在要把這個價格做假，得花多少錢？有多少錢押在這個價格上？它把答案算成一個「安全金額」：押的錢比它少，就照價結算；比它多，就拒絕。

At 23:25 on Sunday, the cheapest fake was to push the price up about 20%. That would have cost about 4.08 USDG. A 20% fake gains the faker about 0.20 USDG for every 1 USDG riding on the price. So the safe amount was 4.08 ÷ 0.20 ≈ 20.38 USDG.

週日 23:25，最便宜的造假方式是把價格推高約 20%，只要約 4.08 USDG。價格被假造 20%，押在上面的每 1 USDG，造假者約能賺 0.20。所以安全金額是 4.08 ÷ 0.20 ≈ 20.38 USDG。

This pool never had HAKARI. We replayed that weekend's real trades through HAKARI's rules, minute by minute, so this is a what-if replay, not a record. In the replay, the safe amount was about 7,259 USDG at 19:40 on Sunday. By 23:25, it had fallen to about 20.

So, in the replay, a payout worth 1,000 USDG would have been refused for 183 minutes, about three hours in all. The longest stretch ran two hours without a break, from 22:51 to 00:50. In those same minutes, our own first, simpler rule would have paid out at prices up to 51.70. That is nearly 80% above Friday's close. We have since replaced that rule.

Even on a calm evening, this small pool could safely carry only about 7,259 USDG in the replay. So a 10,000 USDG payout would have been refused anyway. That is the pool's normal size, not the squeeze.
