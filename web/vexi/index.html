A live options venue, every fix checked against the bound

Our own venue on testnet 46630 (built before the hackathon; none of its code is in this repo) sells physically settled 15-minute options and fixes each settlement price once, from a 300-second TWAP of its own hookless Uniswap v4 pool on the official PoolManager. Because every contract on an expiry is an ERC-6909 token with public supply, the exposure settling on that pool's price is readable for this one consumer, and HAKARI's bound can be checked against every fix the venue has made.

What this is not: a market. The tokens are mocks, every pool holds one full-range position we seeded, all open interest was written by one address (the venue's own) and all swaps came from its own swap adapter. What it shows is the pipeline on a real consumer's contracts, and one result about rate limiters.

What the six pools can carry now

Per pricing pool: the largest settlement its price can safely carry with nobody pushing back (CostModel.maxSafeExposure, arbReversionSeconds = 0), at the binding rung and at the smallest one, next to fee × the pool's quote-side reserve. A settlement at or above the bound would be refused; from half of it the board says watch. The "now" columns are one read per pool of the deployed PushCostLens and the PoolManager's own storage on the public testnet RPC, made when this page loaded; the rest is the collector's last run.

What you are looking at

  • S* is the venue's own fix: a rate-limited ring of a handful of TWAP observations over the last 300 s before expiry. The limiter clips a big atomic push and passes a small push held through the window untouched; on a single full-range position the cost per move is nearly the same at every rung, so the limiter does not raise the safe line above fee × reserve.
  • arbReversionSeconds = 0 because no third-party swap has been seen on these pools: the bound assumes nobody pushes back, which is the conservative setting, not a measurement.
  • HAKARI discloses; it cannot gate the venue's fix. Nothing in the venue reads the bound, and a fix never reverts. The history above is a replay of what the bound would have said.
