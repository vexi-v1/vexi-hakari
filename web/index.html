1 · The HIMS weekend, replayed from events

Sunday 2026-08-30, Robinhood's busiest day on-chain ($270.6M of stock-token volume, per SQD) and a day with no minting. With nobody able to arbitrage, the cost to push HIMS/USDG 10 % fell from 1,351 USDG at 19:40 UTC to 12 USDG at 23:53 UTC, by when the pool already read 43.27 against a 28.84 NYSE close. It peaked at 54.50 as minting reopened, and fell back to 29.31 after the first mint. The largest settlement the pool could safely carry fell from 7,259 USDG to 115: SafeSettle would have refused anything above that. Positions rebuilt from every ModifyLiquidity log; at each point the rebuilt liquidity equals the last Swap event's own liquidity field.

2 · Was HIMS a one-off? Every stock pool, one weekend

The deepest USDG pool of each Robinhood stock token, rebuilt from logs from Friday's US close to Monday, priced as the largest settlement it could safely carry with nobody pushing back. On the weekend of 2026-09-18 nothing collapsed: the weekend low was 0.69× to 2.4× of Friday (HIMS 1.06×); on 2026-08-30 HIMS fell to 0.016× (7,259 → 115 USDG). A closed mint window makes a collapse possible, not certain, which is why the bound is measured at settlement.

3 · Measure any pool, right now

Any v4 pool on the official PoolManager. The page simulates the lens's deployment, injects it with an eth_call state override, and asks mainnet live. Nothing is deployed.

4 · Cost to push: a snapshot

Cost = what an attacker loses pushing the price by X and selling straight back (fees on both legs). Capital = the input the push needs, returned afterwards. In USDG.

5 · SafeSettle's decisions

Each row is one settlement from the test suite. SafeSettle prices faking a move of 0.5–20 % (and the gap between the two TWAPs, if any) from the pool's price now, both ways, over the window, re-pushing after every pull-back when arbitrage is open; the smallest cost ÷ gain is the max safe exposure. settle on raw when the total exposure is below it, refuse otherwise. Agreeing TWAPs are not evidence: a push held long enough makes them agree.
